Reading the estimates

Publication, stability, and interpretation

01

Coverage first

County estimates are generally published when at least 80% of officers are at agencies with one or more credible reporting years during 2023–2025. Approved Florida and California sources supply the 2025 county component where a temporal imputation would otherwise be used; the agency table retains the temporal estimates. A non-sheriff agency-year with fewer than one corrected arrest per full-time officer is treated as having no usable report.

02

Process-noise uncertainty

Each metric has an approximate 90% credible interval based on an NB2 model for ordinary year-to-year count variation. A metric is visually suppressed on the map when its coefficient of variation exceeds 30%. These intervals do not include uncertainty from reporting corrections, source translation, or imputation.

03

Robust comparison

Each metric’s percentiles and quartiles are defined using published counties whose NB2 coefficient of variation is no greater than 30%. Higher-uncertainty estimates are then located within that reference distribution without influencing it.

04

Race-specific rates

Black arrest numerators use directly reported Black-race shares when available and validated estimates otherwise. Black denominators are Black-alone ACS residents; White denominators are non-Hispanic White-alone residents.

These are statistical estimates, not exact counts. Use county cautions and agency provenance to understand how much modeling or data transformation contributes to a result. Read the full technical methods.
